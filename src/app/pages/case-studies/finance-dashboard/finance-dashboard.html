01 // ARCHITECTURAL CONTEXT

Pure Personal Utility

Managing personal capital frequently suffers from two extremes: over-bloated legacy banking utilities buried under opaque menus, or crude spreadsheets lacking instant visual telemetry.

This project was engineered to deliver immediate clarity: real-time balance aggregation, visual expense trajectory curves, and zero-friction transaction ingestion.

account_balance_wallet KPI MODULE 01

Executive KPI Cards

Instantaneous calculation of Net Total Balance, Monthly Inflow, and Total Burn with color-coded positive/negative delta indicators.

show_chart KPI MODULE 02

Continuous Telemetry

Bivariate interactive visual chart comparing cashflow trajectories, income milestones, and expense spikes across billing cycles.

pie_chart KPI MODULE 03

Expense Breakdown

Categorical allocation mapping identifying proportional distribution across Housing, Food, Subscriptions, Utilities, and Discretionary costs.

receipt_long KPI MODULE 04

Live Transaction Ledger

Real-time tabular transaction history with search, category filtering, and instant deletion mutability.

02 // INTERFACE ENGINEERING

Data Architecture & Visualization

How raw accounting inputs are converted into an instant, high-contrast operational dashboard without latency or visual noise.

TELEMETRY_01

Bivariate Charting

Dual-line canvas graphics render income versus expense metrics with smoothed tension curves, tooltips, and dynamic monthly boundaries.

Engine: Chart.js HTML5 Canvas
TELEMETRY_02

Category Proportions

Polar doughnut chart visualizing relative capital outflow with high-contrast palette tagging for intuitive budget surveillance.

Distribution: Percentage auto-normalizer
TELEMETRY_03

Zero-Friction Ingestion

Lightweight ingestion modal configured with quick-select categories, automated numerical formatting, and optimistic state updates.

Latency: Sub-50ms local render
